Research Focus

My work centres on making vision and language models accurate, efficient, and deployable. Recent directions include vision-language models for document layout analysis and structured extraction (Doc2Tag, ViLLA, receipt OCR), real-time detection and instance segmentation for retail and transportation, gaze estimation and gaze-on-object prediction, thermal image super-resolution, and lightweight architectures for Jetson-class hardware.

How can we build vision-language and perception models that are state-of-the-art in accuracy yet efficient enough to run reliably on constrained, real-world hardware?
