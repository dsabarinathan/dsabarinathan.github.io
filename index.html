I am a Senior AI Engineer and computer-vision research engineer with 10+ years of experience building deep-learning systems that ship — from vision-language models for Japanese document understanding to lightweight detection and segmentation networks running in real time on edge hardware. My work spans vision-language models, object detection & segmentation, OCR & document AI, gaze estimation, and efficient models for Jetson-class devices. I have authored 17+ peer-reviewed papers at venues including CVPR/ICCV/ECCV workshops, IEEE TMI, and Engineering Applications of AI, and I am a Kaggle Competitions Expert with a Gold medal (Top 1%) and multiple top-3 finishes in international computer-vision challenges.

Research

My research and engineering focus on making vision and language models practical: accurate, efficient, and deployable in the real world. Recent directions include vision-language models for document layout analysis and structured extraction (Doc2Tag, ViLLA, receipt OCR), real-time object detection and instance segmentation for retail and transportation, gaze estimation and gaze-on-object prediction, thermal image super-resolution, and lightweight architectures for edge devices such as Jetson Nano and Xavier.

How can we build vision-language and perception models that are both state-of-the-art in accuracy and efficient enough to run reliably on constrained, real-world hardware?
